Case Study / 04

Travel planning.

Overview

Nomad is a low-cost travel planner made to make it easy, clear, and visually appealing to plan trips. Nomad consolidates crucial trip information into a single responsive dashboard, saving travellers from having to manage locations, costs, timetables, maps, and memories across numerous disparate applications. Clear, high-priority cards that adjust to desktop, tablet, and mobile displays are used by the interface to display information using a modular Bento Grid architecture. Goal: Organise trip preparation without making it seem difficult.

02 / Research

Understanding how people plan trips.

The biggest opportunity lies between Budget Estimation and Daily Planning. Users frequently ask: "Can I actually afford this trip before I commit to detailed day-to-day planning?"

03 / User Journey

To establish a solid UX foundation, research focused on mapping the end-to-end traveler journey to identify primary drop-off and friction points: Inspiration ➔ Choose Destination ➔ Estimate Budget ➔ Plan Transport ➔ Book Accommodation ➔ Create Itinerary ➔ Track Expenses ➔ Travel ➔ Save Memories
